    Where the Earth came from

    The globe is NASA Blue Marble by day and NASA Black Marble 2016 by night, with NASA Visible Earth clouds and GEBCO relief. Up close, Chicago is USGS NAIP aerial photography, its streetlights are every OpenStreetMap road within ten kilometres of the Loop (ODbL), and its buildings and facades are Chicago Open World’s own, built from the City of Chicago data portal, OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia’s list of tallest buildings. Melbourne and southern England are Sentinel-2 cloudless by EOX IT Services (CC BY 4.0, contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016), with Melbourne’s streets from OpenStreetMap. Hong Kong is aerial photography and mapping from the Lands Department of the HKSAR Government. Regional night lights are NASA Black Marble at 500 m from GIBS. Albert Park is traced from bacinger/f1-circuits (MIT). The 9,096 stars are the Yale Bright Star Catalogue, turned to where they stand tonight, and the sun is placed with NOAA’s solar position equations.

    Built by hand with Three.js and a few hundred lines of GLSL. No trackers. Source on GitHub.
